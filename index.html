Your Shopify sale is invisible to Google. Here is the snippet that fixes it.

Google turns an active price into a sale price only when a second price, marked priceType: https://schema.org/StrikethroughPrice, is also supplied. Shopify's default theme does not emit it — so a discount your own catalog records is invisible to Google.

Why your discount does not show up, and why it is not your mistake

Shopify's default theme renders its product structured data through one Shopify-controlled Liquid filter. That filter's own documented output does not include a strikethrough price. Every store on a standard theme inherits the same gap — which is why one snippet fixes it the same way on almost any of them.

  1. Save snippets/archer-merchant-listing.liquid into your theme.
  2. In sections/main-product.liquid, comment out the theme's own JSON-LD block. Two competing Product nodes on one page is worse than one — Google picks, and it may not pick yours.
  3. In the same place, render the snippet with your product object.
snippets/archer-merchant-listing.liquid

Full install instructions, verification steps and the optional sale window are in the repository README.

What it deliberately does not do

  • It never invents a sale. No compare_at_price above price means no strikethrough spec and no sale window. The page stays truthful.
  • It never emits a priceValidUntil in the past.
  • It emits nothing at all when the active price is not greater than zero — silence is the honest output for a $0.00 product.
  • It covers the selected variant only. Multi-variant ProductGroup markup is out of scope.
